# Brag Plan: BrandGauge

## What is this app?
BrandGauge is a brand intelligence platform for Nigerian and West African marketing teams: it
reads sentiment in Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa, measures channels global tools ignore
(billboards, field activations, events), and resolves all of it into one defensible score.

## The angle
Every brand tool in the world can read English. The thing BrandGauge actually does that nobody
else does is understand that "e don burst" is a compliment and "this brand no try" is a
condemnation. That is not a tagline, it is a lookup table sitting in the repo
(`src/lib/ai/prompts/sentiment.ts`) that the classifier uses every night. So the video shows the
product reading its market in its own language, then collapses that into the gauge. The angle is
quiet confidence: no hype, just the thing nobody else can do, then the number.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The product's own hero line, on the Ink ground, with the brand's tick-field behind it:
**"See your brand the way the street sees it."** The word *street* lands in Danfo yellow, exactly
as the landing page sets it. No logo yet. The line is the hook.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Three real mentions arrive and get read.** "E don burst", "This brand no try", "Omo, e sweet
  die" — verbatim from the project's own expression reference — each resolving to its correct
  reading (positive / negative / positive). The product doing the one thing that is hard.
- **The needle settles.** The real BHI gauge, drawn by the project's own `brand/engine.js`, sweeps
  and settles on 67 / Healthy. Not a picture of a gauge: the shipped gauge.
- **The channels nobody else counts.** A tight row naming what feeds the number, including the
  offline half: billboards, field reports, events, surveys.

## Outro / punchline
The payoff is the product's own promise, which is a budget-meeting promise, not a marketing one:
**"One number you can defend in a budget meeting."** Then the lockup, then "Start free in beta".

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result, as a marketer actually experiences it:
1. Mentions arrive from the connected platforms, in the languages they were written in.
2. Each is classified: sentiment label and cultural reading.
3. The Brand Health Index moves, and the zone changes. That is the screen they show their CMO.
Scenes 2 and 3 are this flow. Scene 4 frames it; scenes 1 and 5 bookend it.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a quiet premium product film spoken in the brand's own ink-and-Flare language
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer holds, one hero plane per frame. Motion settles rather than
  bounces, matching the design system's own motion law. Confidence comes from restraint: no
  exclamation, no count-up gimmick except the one the product itself performs (the needle).

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 21.0s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#16120E` (Ink, `--bg-ink`)
- Accent: `#FF3D14` (Flare, `--flare`) with `#FFC12E` (Danfo, `--tx-flare` on ink) for the hook accent
- Text: `#FAF6EF` (`--tx-inv`), secondary `#A9A299` (`--tx-inv-2`)
- Positive: `#9BC98A` (`--pos` lifted for Ink). Negative: `#FF3D14`.
- Display font: Nohemi (local `brand/fonts/Nohemi-{400,500,700,800}.woff2`)
- Body font: Nohemi. Numerals: Disket Mono (`brand/fonts/DisketMono-{400,700}.woff2`), tabular, for
  the BHI reading only.
- Strongest visual element: the BHI gauge — seven ticks on a 163.3° → 2.4° sweep with a rigid
  needle, generated by `brand/engine.js` (`GEOM`, `sweep()`), plus the brand tick-field.

## Design-system rules this video must obey (from `brand/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` + CLAUDE.md)
- Flare fills whole planes, once per frame. It is never outline and never body text.
- Type on a Flare plane is Ink (`--on-hot`), never Paper.
- Elevation is a one-pixel hairline at `--line-inv`, never a shadow.
- Radius: cards 4px, controls 3px, pills round. Nothing else is rounded.
- Deltas read `+` and `−`. No triangles — Disket has none and the licence forbids adding them.
- Banned motion: anything that loops, numbers counting up, hover lift, bounce. The needle settles.
- Mention text stays verbatim, unglossed and unitalicised in every language.
- Voice: no em dashes. Banned words: delve, underscore, pivotal, crucial, robust, vibrant,
  leverage, seamless, tapestry.

## Grounding notes
Every line on screen is project material. The hook and the budget-meeting line are verbatim from
`src/components/landing/landing-page.tsx`. The three mentions and their readings are verbatim rows
from the expression reference in `src/lib/ai/prompts/sentiment.ts`. The zone names and thresholds
are from `src/lib/bhi.ts`. The channel list is from `BRANDGAUGE_OVERVIEW.md`.
**Illustrative stand-in:** the reading of 67 is demo data, not a measured customer score. It sits
inside the real Healthy band (65–80) and no customer, handle or person is named anywhere.

## Share copy (draft)
BrandGauge reads your market in Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa, counts the billboards too, and
hands you one number you can defend in a budget meeting.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (109.96 BPM, calm, unhurried)
- Music treatment: in at 0.0 around 0.5 volume, holds under the whole piece, fades from 19.6s so
  the final lockup lands in near-silence. No ducking needed (no narration).
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read at
  `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`.
  Strong cues to target: **12.562s** (the needle settling, the video's biggest moment) and
  **15.824s** (the channel row completing). Beat grid for the sequential mentions:
  4.64 / 5.74 / 6.82 (every other beat, so each line gets 1.1s to be read).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle. Use RMS to breathe the tick-field density and a soft warmth
  behind the gauge. No waveform, no equalizer, no pulsing on text.
- SFX posture: sparse. Three or four cues in the whole video.
- Audio-coupled moments: each mention chip arriving; the needle reaching its reading; the lockup.
- Restraint rule: nothing comedic, nothing percussive on text, no riser, no whoosh stack. If a cue
  does not match real on-screen motion, leave it out.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The street — 4.1s
Ink ground. The brand tick-field sits well behind, masked to a soft radial, exactly as the landing
page holds it. The hero line sets in Nohemi at display size, centred:
"See your brand the way the street sees it." — *street* in Danfo `#FFC12E`.
The line arrives as two mask-wipes (not a fade-and-slide-up, which the system bans), fast in over
0.45s, then holds settled for ~2.9s. The eyebrow "Brand intelligence that reads your market in its
own language" sits above it in Danfo at small size, arriving first.
Sequential/interaction: the eyebrow lands, then the headline wipes in. No cursor.
Audio intent: the bed establishes, quiet and unhurried. Nothing else.
Audio-coupled idea: none. Let the hook sit in the music alone.
Music: calm, low.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — It reads what they actually wrote — 5.2s
The centrepiece. Ink ground, hairline-ruled card well. Three mention chips arrive one by one, each
a Paper-on-Ink raised card (`#221D18`, 4px radius, hairline `--line-inv`), each carrying verbatim
mention text and, 0.35s later, its resolved reading on the right:
- "E don burst" → Positive · Excellent, very impressive
- "This brand no try" → Negative · Strong condemnation
- "Omo, e sweet die" → Positive · Extremely enjoyable
Readings use `--pos` `#9BC98A` and `--neg` `#FF3D14` as filled status pills with the word, never
hue alone. Mention text stays unitalicised and unglossed.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three cards, beat-gridded at 4.64 / 5.74 / 6.82, each holding 1.1s
before the next, with all three held together for the final 1.4s of the scene.
Audio intent: each arrival gets a small, dry, non-musical card cue. Low high-frequency risk.
Audio-coupled idea: card sequence — one soft cue per arrival, landing with the motion, not after.
Music: steady, unchanged.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The needle settles — 4.9s
The gauge, generated by the project's own `brand/engine.js` so the arc, tick growth and needle are
the shipped geometry, not a redraw. The needle sweeps from rest and **settles** on its reading over
~1.1s with no overshoot, landing at 12.562s. The reading sets below in Disket Mono, tabular: **67**,
with the zone pill "Healthy" in `--pos`. A hairline caption reads "Brand Health Index".
Lit ticks carry the Danfo → Ember → Flare ramp the engine computes; unlit ticks stay ink at 16%.
Sequential/interaction: the needle travel is the interaction. No counting-up numerals: the reading
sets once, when the needle arrives.
Audio intent: one restrained announcement cue at the settle. This is the payoff.
Audio-coupled idea: needle settle beat-locked to the 12.562s strong cue.
Music: holds. A touch more presence under the gauge via RMS.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Including the half nobody counts — 3.3s
Four short labels in one row, arriving left to right, naming what feeds the number:
"Social · Billboards · Field reports · Surveys", under a single line:
"More than half the budget goes to channels Google Analytics cannot see."
One Flare plane enters behind the row as the hero of this frame.
Sequential/interaction: yes — four labels on consecutive beats, completing on the 15.824s cue, then
the whole row holds 1.1s.
Audio intent: ticks only, very quiet, matching each label.
Audio-coupled idea: beat-grid the four labels; no cue on the sentence.
Music: lifts slightly into the final cue.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — The budget meeting — 3.5s
Ink. The line sets alone, large, held long:
"One number you can defend in a budget meeting."
It holds 1.7s, then clears to the BrandGauge lockup (`brand/logo/brandgauge-lockup-duotone-paper.svg`)
centred, with "Start free in beta" small beneath it in Danfo.
Sequential/interaction: none. A long hold is the point.
Audio intent: music fades from 19.6s. The lockup lands in near-silence with one soft, low cue.
Audio-coupled idea: the lockup arrival.
Music: fading out.
Transition mood: the end.

**Scene durations:** 4.1 + 5.2 + 4.9 + 3.3 + 3.5 = **21.0s** ✓ (inside 15–25s)
**Music mood for this video:** calm, warm, unhurried
**Audio summary:** One quiet bed holds the whole film, three dry card cues mark the mentions
arriving, one restrained cue marks the needle settling, and the music clears before the lockup so
the last line lands in near-silence.
