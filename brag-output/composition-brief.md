## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: polished
- Creative direction: a quiet premium product film spoken in the brand's own ink-and-Flare language
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer holds, one hero plane per frame, motion that settles.
- Angle: every brand tool can read English. BrandGauge knows "e don burst" is a compliment. Show
  the product reading its market in its own language, then collapse it into the number.
- Hook: the hero line on Ink with *street* in Danfo, over the brand tick-field.
- Outro: "One number you can defend in a budget meeting." then the lockup.
- Avoid: generic SaaS language, abstract filler, any redesign of the brand, em dashes in copy,
  the banned words (delve, underscore, pivotal, crucial, robust, vibrant, leverage, seamless,
  tapestry), counting-up numerals, bounce, loops, hover lift, shadows for elevation.

## Audio
- Audio role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Audio arc: bed establishes under the hook, holds through the mentions and the gauge, lifts
  slightly into the channel row, fades from 19.6s so the lockup lands in near-silence.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: volume ~0.5 throughout, fade out 19.6s → 21.0s. No ducking (no narration).
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset at
  `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`.
  Lock the needle settle to the **12.562s** strong cue. Second lock available at **15.824s** for
  the channel row completing. Beat-grid the three mention cards at 4.64 / 5.74 / 6.82 (every other
  beat, so each line holds 1.1s and stays readable).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle. RMS breathes the tick-field and a soft warmth behind the gauge.
  No waveform, no equalizer, no text scaling.
- Audio-coupled moments:
  - Scene 2, each card arriving — dry card cue, landing with the motion
  - Scene 3, the needle settling — one restrained announcement cue, the payoff
  - Scene 5, the lockup — one soft low cue over the faded bed
- SFX selection guidance: sparse, three or four cues total. Low high-frequency risk throughout:
  this is a polished tone and the cues repeat. Nothing comedic, no riser, no whoosh.
- SFX analysis guidance: read `assets/sfx/sfx-analysis.md` in the brag skill directory before
  choosing files; prefer the low-HF-risk picks.
- Exact SFX choice: Hyperframes chooses filenames and timestamps once the animation exists.
- Audio files: copy the chosen music and SFX into `brag-output/composition/assets/`.
