# BrandGauge fix list (3 November 2026)

Two checks: one read all the code, one used the live app as a new customer.

**The short version:** the app is not broken everywhere. All 63 dashboard pages load, the phone layout holds, and the tours work. The problems are in a few places. Most take under half a day each to fix.

---

## 1. Fix before any real brand signs up

| # | What's wrong | The fix | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Survey emails show everyone's address.** Up to 50 people in one "To" line. A privacy (NDPR) problem. | Send one email per person. | Small |
| 2 | **Signup is a dead end.** New users land on "Welcome back, sign in", then see "Email not confirmed". | Show a "Check your email" screen. | Small |
| 3 | **Every brand is saved as FMCG.** A fintech brand gets FMCG scoring. | Onboarding sets the brand type from the industry they pick. | Small |
| 4 | **A paywall shows in the free beta.** Billing shows a trial and prices. The Client Portal allows 0 links. | Hide Billing. Remove the portal limit. | Small |
| 5 | **X stops working after the first night.** | Save the new login token X sends back. | Small |
| 6 | **Survey panels never send.** | Fix the status value, and give panel surveys their questions. | Small |
| 7 | **Google sign-in creates an empty extra workspace** every time, for anyone in two or more workspaces. | Fix the lookup that thinks they are a new user. | Small |
| 8 | **Business Case invents a story from zero data** ("invest now or lose the market"). | Show "not enough data yet" until there is data. | Small |
| 9 | **"Connected" when it isn't.** Mailchimp, Brevo and App Store accept any key. Meta Ads stays "Connected" while its syncs fail. | Check the key before saving. Show sync errors on the card. | Small |
| 10 | **One brand shows another brand's X or Instagram as connected.** | Filter by brand. | Small |

## 2. Fix before Meta and Google app review

| What's wrong | The fix | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Meta's data-deletion callback records nothing. **Meta review needs this.** | Make it save, and add the status page it promises. | Small |
| GA4's daily sync saves nothing, and no screen shows GA4 numbers. | Fix the save, then show the numbers. | Small, then medium |
| Meta Ads spend is always labelled naira, even for dollar accounts. | Use the account's own currency. | Small |
| Instagram, Facebook and Meta Ads connect the *first* Page or ad account, not always the right one. | Ask which one. | Medium |
| Instagram reach and impressions read 0 (Meta retired those metrics). Logins expire after 60 days without warning. | Use the current metrics. Warn before expiry. | Small + medium |
| Google Ads uses an old API version Google has switched off. | Update it when you get the developer token. | Small |

## 3. Quick polish (about a day in total)

- **Digital Ads page never finishes loading.** Google Ads and X Ads buttons fail silently. Mark them "Coming soon".
- **A/B Testing list errors** behind the scenes. The page wrongly says "No experiments yet".
- **Retention shows "Low risk"** with no data at all.
- **Customers see technical setting names** on the WhatsApp, Meta Ads and Campaign Audit screens.
- **Competitor Tracker gives up entirely** if the competitor's site blocks us. It should still read the press, the app and X.
- **Brand Gap leads with a 2024 story** marked "high confidence".
- **Market Demand shows a raw code error** when something goes wrong.
- **Em dashes** in page titles and some pages. Off-brand 404 page.

## 4. Leave for later

- AI cost tracking per feature. Only 1 of 52 AI calls records which feature it came from. It doesn't affect customers.
- Product tours missing on about 12 smaller pages.
- WhatsApp sending limits. WhatsApp isn't live yet.
- LinkedIn, TikTok and X Ads. Hidden or unbuilt. Just don't offer them.

---

**How to go about it:** do section 1 in one go. That's about one to two days of my work, then one deploy. Section 2 comes next, before you apply for Meta review. Section 3 can ride along with either.

**Not checked live:** anything behind a real Google, Meta or X login, anything that sends email, and Market Demand (the test environment cut it off). Full details, if you ever want them: `docs/audits/2026-11-03-code-audit.md` and `docs/audits/2026-11-03-live-audit.md`.
